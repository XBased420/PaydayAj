About

Dallas made him. Blessed & Determined is the whole plan, printed on everything he drops.

Payday AJ is a Dallas artist, actor and independent operator who spent years putting out singles and visuals before turning it into a debut album. BIGGEST PAYDAY EVER landed on Halloween 2025 — thirteen tracks, a Texas Powerball ticket on the cover, and no label behind it.

Between releases he's on set: short films with Dallas FilmHub and Cliffwoods, a lead in IRM Films' The One, interviews with the people who decide what Dallas hears next.

Music, film, and the business behind both — run out of one city, on his own terms.
